# Code of Conduct

Private Translator contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, languages, and experience levels.
- Give specific technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's translation content, logs, or identifying data without permission.
- Accept correction and help make decisions understandable to future contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory form when public discussion would expose personal or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
